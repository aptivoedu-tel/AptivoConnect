# Validation Metrics

## Proposed first 90-day targets (treat as hypotheses, not forecasts)

- 10–20 approved active creators
- 30–50 reviewed products
- 100+ first-time paying buyers
- 200+ completed paid orders
- 3–5% conversion among qualified product-page visitors
- Refund rate below 5%
- 20%+ repeat purchase rate within 30 days
- 100% of eligible creator payouts settled or explicitly flagged for a documented issue
- Zero unresolved serious content-accuracy issues

## Track weekly

- Qualified product views
- Preview-to-purchase conversion
- Average order value
- Repeat purchases
- Active selling creators
- Creator first-sale rate
- Refunds
- Payment failures
- Content reports
- Payout timeliness
- Contribution margin after transaction-level costs

## North-star metric

**Weekly successful paid learning transactions**, excluding failed, refunded, or reversed orders.
